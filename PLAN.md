**Product Name:** EdgeNoted

### 1. Product Overview
EdgeNoted is a lightweight, always-available utility designed for zero-friction access to notes and reminders. It slides in from the screen edge, lets you view and edit content instantly, and hides again so you stay in flow without switching apps.

**Core Value Proposition:**
“Notes and reminders without app switching.” EdgeNoted stays on top of other applications (including full-screen mode and Stage Manager), can be invoked instantly via keyboard shortcut, Open Bar click, or mouse-to-edge (Hot Side mode), and disappears when not needed.

**Key Twist:**
EdgeNoted does not store its own notes. It opens and edits notes directly from the Apple Notes app with **bi-directional synchronization**. Only a **single note** can be displayed at a time. It also provides full support for items from the Apple Reminders app.

**Target Users:**
Knowledge workers, students, developers, designers, writers, and anyone who wants rapid access to their existing Apple Notes and Reminders without leaving the current context.

**Positioning:**
A persistent, edge-activated companion that surfaces a single Apple Note or Reminders view as a natural extension of the desktop.

### 2. Core User Experience & Interaction Model
- **Always-on-Top Behavior:** Remains visible above other apps, including full-screen windows and Stage Manager.
- **Invocation Methods:**
  - Global keyboard shortcut
  - Click the Open Bar
  - Hot Side mode (move mouse cursor to screen edge)
- **Hide Behavior:** Slides away to restore full focus on the primary application.
- **Single-Note Display:** Only one Apple Note is shown and editable at any time.
- **Visual Design:** Clean, minimal. Looks great out of the box; fully themeable.
- **Keyboard-First Workflow:** Designed for rapid, keyboard-driven use.

### 3. Feature Specification

#### 3.1 Apple Notes Integration (Bi-Directional)
- Opens and edits notes sourced exclusively from the Apple Notes app.
- Full **bi-directional sync**: changes made in EdgeNoted appear immediately in Apple Notes, and changes made in Apple Notes appear in EdgeNoted.
- Only a single note is displayed and editable at a time.
- Quick switching between notes while preserving the single-note view.
- Folders from Apple Notes are respected for organization and navigation.
- Pin Notes & Folders (keep important items at the top of the list).
- Reordering support within the EdgeNoted interface.
- Fold Notes (collapse long notes to save vertical space; expand on demand).
- Searching across Apple Notes.

#### 3.2 Apple Reminders Support
- View, create, edit, and complete reminders from the Apple Reminders app.
- Bi-directional sync with Apple Reminders.
- Support for lists, due dates, priorities, and completion status.
- Quick capture of new reminders from within EdgeNoted.

#### 3.3 Organization & Navigation
- Folders (sourced from Apple Notes).
- Quick move between folders.
- Reordering of notes and folders.
- Searching.

#### 3.4 Content Capabilities
- **Images:** Embed or view screenshots and images already present in Apple Notes.
- **File & Folder Shortcuts:** Create or open shortcuts to important files or folders.
- **Color Code Preview:** Enter and preview colors in #rrggbb format (useful for developers/designers).
- **Tasks & Checklists:** Native support (aligned with Apple Notes / Reminders task behavior).

#### 3.5 Appearance & Customization
- Built-in themes + ability to create custom themes.
- Themes & customization store/page available.
